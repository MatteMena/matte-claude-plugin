# Sign up and sign in

Signing up takes under 2 minutes and starts a **7-day free trial with no credit card needed**.

---

## Create an account

1. Go to [nerve.mattemena.com](https://nerve.mattemena.com) and choose the **Sign up** tab.
2. Either click **Continue with Google**, or enter your **Work email** and a **Password** (at least 8 characters).
3. Complete the security check (CAPTCHA) and click **Start free trial**.
4. If you used email, Nerve sends a 6-digit code. Enter it on the **Check your email** screen and click **Verify & start trial**.
   - Didn't get it? Click **Resend code**. You can request a new code every 30 seconds.
   - Typed the wrong address? Click **Use a different email**.
5. You're taken to [create your first workspace](create-workspace.md).

:::tip Invited by a teammate?
Sign up with the **same email address** the invitation was sent to. Pending invitations are accepted automatically when you sign in, and the workspace appears in your workspace switcher.
:::

---

## Sign in

1. Go to [nerve.mattemena.com](https://nerve.mattemena.com) and choose the **Sign in** tab.
2. Click **Continue with Google**, or enter your **Work email** and **Password** and click **Sign in**.
3. If you have two-step verification turned on, enter the code on the **Verify it's you** screen and click **Verify**.

You land on the **Overview** page of your workspace.

---

## Reset your password

1. On the **Sign in** tab, enter your email first.
2. Click **Forgot password?**.
3. Enter the 6-digit **Verification code** from your email and a **New password** (at least 8 characters).
4. Click **Reset password**.

---

## Sign out

Click your avatar in the top-right corner and choose **Sign out**.

---

## Troubleshooting

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Couldn't find your account. Sign up to get started with Nerve." | There's no account with that email. Use the **Sign up** tab, or check for typos. |
| "An account with this email already exists. Sign in instead." | Use the **Sign in** tab. If you've forgotten your password, use **Forgot password?**. |
| "Incorrect password. Try again or reset it." | Retype it, or reset it with **Forgot password?**. |
| "That code isn't right…" | Check the latest email; older codes stop working once a new one is sent. |
| "That code has expired. Request a new one." | Click **Resend code**. |
| "Enter your email first, then tap "Forgot password?"." | Fill in the email field before clicking **Forgot password?**. |
| No verification email | Check spam/promotions, wait 30 seconds, then click **Resend code**. |
