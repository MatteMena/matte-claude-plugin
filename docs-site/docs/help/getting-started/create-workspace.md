# Create a workspace

A **workspace** is where you and your team manage connected accounts, workflows, AI agents and billing. Each workspace has its own plan, credits, members and connections. Agencies often create one workspace per client.

---

## Create your first workspace

After you sign up, Nerve asks you to create a workspace.

1. On the **Create your workspace** screen, enter a **Workspace name** (for example, "My Agency"). Use a name your team will recognize.
2. Click **Create workspace**.
3. Nerve automatically starts a **free Agency trial**: 7 days and 200 credits, with no card needed. You'll see the message *"Your free Agency trial has started."*
4. You're taken to the **Overview** page.

:::note
Each email address gets **one** free trial, across all the workspaces it creates. If your trial has already been used, Nerve takes you to **Credits & Billing** to choose a plan instead. See [Free trial](../billing/free-trial.md).
:::

---

## The Overview page

**Overview** is your home page. It shows:

- **Setup progress**: a 3-step checklist (*0 of 3 steps done*). Each step is also in the sidebar under **Get started**:
  1. **Connect accounts**: [connect your ad platforms](../accounts/connect-accounts.md)
  2. **Connect to AI**: add Nerve to [Claude](../connect-ai/claude.md) or [ChatGPT](../connect-ai/chatgpt.md)
  3. **First prompt**: [run a starter prompt](first-prompt.md)
- **Connected platforms**: the platforms you've connected, with a **View all** link.
- **Next steps in your AI assistant**: links to the **Claude guide** and **ChatGPT guide**.
- **What you've unlocked**: a summary of the features available to you.

:::info
Only step 1 is ticked off automatically. Nerve can't detect when you've connected an AI assistant or run a prompt, so steps 2 and 3 stay unticked.
:::

---

## Create another workspace

1. Click the **workspace switcher** at the bottom of the sidebar.
2. Click **+ Create workspace**.
3. Enter a name and click **Create workspace**.

A new workspace needs its own plan. The free trial can only be used once per email, so further workspaces need a paid plan. See [Plans and pricing](../billing/plans-and-pricing.md).

---

## Switch between workspaces

1. Click the **workspace switcher** at the bottom of the sidebar.
2. If you have several, type in **Search workspaces…**.
3. Click a workspace. Your role (Owner, Admin, Member or Viewer) is shown next to each workspace, and the current one has a check mark.

You're taken to that workspace's **Overview**.

## Common questions

**Can I rename or delete a workspace?**
Not in the app yet. Email [hello@mattemena.com](mailto:hello@mattemena.com) from the owner's email address.

**What happens if my workspace has no active plan?**
Every page except **Credits & Billing** is locked until you choose a plan or add a payment method. See [Invoices, cancelling and failed payments](../billing/invoices-cancel-and-payment-failure.md).

**"Failed to create workspace"**
Try again. If it keeps failing, contact support with the workspace name you used.
