# Run your first prompt

After you've [connected your ad accounts](../accounts/connect-accounts.md) and [connected Nerve to Claude](../connect-ai/claude.md), run a starter prompt to see it work.

---

## Steps

1. In the sidebar, go to **Get started** → **First prompt** (*Step 3 of 3, Run your first prompt*).
2. The **Starter prompt** card shows **Analyze my campaigns**.
3. Under **Fill in your details**, type your brand or advertiser name (for example, "Atelier Aroumare").
4. Optional: click **View full prompt** to read the whole prompt before you send it.
5. Either:
   - click **Open in Claude** to open a new Claude chat with the prompt filled in, or
   - click **Copy** and paste the prompt into Claude or ChatGPT.
6. Send the prompt. Claude uses your Nerve connection to pull live data from your connected accounts and analyse it.

---

## What to expect

- The page's **What Claude does with this** section explains what the prompt does.
- **No changes without your approval.** Analysis prompts only read data. If you later ask the AI to change a campaign, it asks you to confirm before it does.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Claude says it can't access your accounts | Check the Nerve connector is enabled in that chat. See [Connect Nerve to Claude](../connect-ai/claude.md#troubleshooting). |
| Claude says no platforms are connected | Connect at least one platform under **Connect accounts**. |
| Claude shows a "Plan required" or "Trial ended" card | Your workspace plan is inactive. See [Invoices, cancelling and failed payments](../billing/invoices-cancel-and-payment-failure.md#when-your-plan-is-inactive). |
