# Help Center

Step-by-step guides for using **Nerve**, the MatteMena web app at [nerve.mattemena.com](https://nerve.mattemena.com). Use Nerve to connect your ad accounts, chat with **Nerve AI**, build automated workflows, track performance, and connect your data to Claude or ChatGPT.

---

## How the app is organised

Everything in Nerve happens inside a **workspace**. The left sidebar groups the pages like this:

| Sidebar section | Pages | What they're for |
|---|---|---|
| — | **Overview** | Your setup progress and connected platforms |
| **Get started** | **Connect accounts**, **Connect to AI**, **First prompt** | The 3-step setup checklist |
| **Planning** | **Prompt library**, **Media planner** | Ready-made prompts and media-plan briefs for Claude |
| **Monitor** | **Analytics** | Cross-platform performance dashboard |
| **AI Agents** | **Agents**, **Workflows** | Chat with Nerve AI, and automate multi-step tasks |
| **Settings** | **Settings**, **Support** | Members, billing, advertiser groups, contact support |

The bottom of the sidebar shows your **credits** and the **workspace switcher**. Your profile and sign-out are under your avatar in the top-right corner.

On mobile, the bottom bar has shortcuts to Overview, Connect accounts and Connect to AI. Every other page is under **More**.
