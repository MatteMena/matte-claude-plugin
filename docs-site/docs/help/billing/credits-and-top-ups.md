# Credits and top-ups

**Credits** pay for the AI work Nerve does inside the app.

---

## What uses credits

| Uses credits | Doesn't use credits |
|---|---|
| [Nerve AI chat](../ai-agents/nerve-ai-chat.md) replies | Using Nerve from **Claude** or **ChatGPT** |
| [Workflow runs](../ai-agents/running-workflows.md), manual or scheduled | Connecting accounts, Analytics, Prompt library, Media planner |

### How much does something cost?
It depends on how much AI work was needed: longer conversations, more steps and more data cost more. A short question might use a few credits; a large multi-platform workflow uses more. You can see the exact cost of every chat reply and run under **Transactions**.

### Failed work is free
If a chat reply or workflow run **fails**, you're **not charged**. It appears as **Credits waived (not charged)**. Retrying a failed run never charges twice.

---

## Where your credits come from

| Type | Amount | Expires? |
|---|---|---|
| **Trial credits** | 200 | Unused trial credits carry over into your first paid month |
| **Subscription credits** | Pro 1,900 / Agency 4,500 each month | Reset each billing month; unused credits don't roll over |
| **Top-up credits** | 500 – 5,000 per pack | **Never expire** |

**Subscription (or trial) credits are used first**, then top-up credits.

---

## Check your balance

- **Sidebar widget**: shows *N credits left*, and turns red when you're at 15% or less.
- **Credits & Billing** → **Credits** tab: **Total remaining**, **Subscription credits** (or **Trial credits**), **Top-up credits — never expire** and the **Billing period**.
- **Transactions** tab: every credit movement:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **AI agent chat** | A Nerve AI reply |
| **Workflow run** | A completed workflow run |
| **Monthly reset** | Your monthly subscription credits were added |
| **Top-up** | You bought a top-up pack |
| **Free trial credits** | Your 200 trial credits |
| **Trial credits carried over** | Unused trial credits added to your first paid month |
| **Subscription credits expired** | Unused credits from last month were removed |
| **Credits waived (not charged)** | A failed run or reply that wasn't charged |

## Buy extra credits

*Owners and Admins. You need an active plan first.*

1. Go to **Credits & Billing** → **Credits**.
2. Click **Buy extra credits**.
3. Pick a pack:

| Pack | Price |
|---|---|
| 500 credits | $5 |
| 1,000 credits | $10 |
| 2,500 credits | $25 |
| 5,000 credits | $50 |

4. Pay on Stripe's checkout page.
5. Back in Nerve: *"Top-up purchased — credits will appear in your balance shortly."*

Top-up receipts appear under **Transactions**, not **Billing history**.

---

## Running out of credits

- New chats and workflow runs are blocked with *"Insufficient credits. Please top up or upgrade your plan."*
- A workflow that's running stops with **"Stopped — out of credits"**. Completed steps are charged, and everyone in the workspace gets an email.
- **Claude and ChatGPT keep working**, because they don't use credits.

To continue, [buy a top-up](#buy-extra-credits), upgrade (see [Plans and pricing](plans-and-pricing.md)), or wait for your monthly reset.

---

## If your subscription ends

Unused **subscription** credits expire. **Top-up** credits are kept, but can only be used once you subscribe again.
