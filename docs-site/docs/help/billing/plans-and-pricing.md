# Plans and pricing

Each **workspace** has its own plan. Prices are per workspace, billed **monthly** in USD.

---

## Plans

| | **Pro** | **Agency** *(Recommended)* | **Enterprise** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Price** | $19 / month | $45 / month | Custom: [talk to us](mailto:hello@mattemena.com) |
| **Credits each month** | 1,900 | 4,500 | Custom |
| **Team members** | 1 | 3 | Unlimited |
| **Different platforms connected** | 3 | 10 | Unlimited |
| **Logins per platform** | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| **Free trial** | 7 days, 200 credits | 7 days, 200 credits | — |
| **Claude & ChatGPT connector** | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Nerve AI chat & workflows** | ✓ (uses credits) | ✓ (uses credits) | ✓ |

- **Credits** are used by Nerve AI chat and workflow runs. Using Nerve from Claude or ChatGPT doesn't use credits. See [Credits and top-ups](credits-and-top-ups.md).
- **Need more credits mid-month?** Buy a [top-up](credits-and-top-ups.md#buy-extra-credits): $5 for 500 credits, and top-up credits never expire.
- **Platform limit** counts different platforms (for example Meta, Google Ads and TikTok = 3). **Logins per platform** is how many separate logins you can connect for one platform; each login can include many ad accounts.

Annual billing isn't available yet.

---

## Choose a plan

Go to **Credits & Billing** → **Plans** and click the plan's button:

| Button | When you'll see it |
|---|---|
| **Get Started** | Starts a [free trial](free-trial.md) |
| **Subscribe** | Starts a paid subscription (trial already used) |
| **Upgrade to …** / **Downgrade to …** | Switch plan during your trial |
| **Resume plan** | Restart a plan after your trial or subscription ended |
| **Current plan** | The plan you're on |

Step-by-step: [Subscribe and manage payment methods](subscribe-and-payment-methods.md).

---

## Changing plans

- **During your trial:** use **Upgrade to …** / **Downgrade to …** on the Plans tab (a saved card is needed). No charge until the trial ends.
- **On a paid plan:** email [hello@mattemena.com](mailto:hello@mattemena.com) and we'll switch you.

---

## Price changes

If prices change, existing subscribers keep the price and monthly credits they signed up with.

---

## Who can manage billing

**Owners and Admins.** Members and Viewers see *"Ask a workspace admin to add a payment method or promo code."*
