# Free trial

Every new Nerve account can try the **Agency** plan free for **7 days**, with **200 credits** and **no credit card required**.

---

## What's included

- Full access to the plan you're trialling, including the Claude and ChatGPT connector
- **200 trial credits** for [Nerve AI chat and workflows](credits-and-top-ups.md)
- The plan's limits apply from day one. For example, Agency allows 3 members and 10 connected platforms.

:::info One trial per email
The free trial can be used **once per email address**, across all workspaces created with it, including deleted ones. If it has been used, plan cards show *"Free trial already used on this account."* and the button says **Subscribe**.
:::

---

## Start your trial

**Automatically:** creating your first workspace starts a free Agency trial. You'll see *"Your free Agency trial has started."*

**Manually** (if you didn't get one automatically):
1. Go to **Credits & Billing** (the credits widget in the sidebar, or **Settings** → **Billing**).
2. On the **Plans** tab, click **Get Started** on a plan.
3. In **Start your free trial**, check *Due today $0.00 · No card required* and the **Trial ends** date.
4. Click **Start free trial**.

---

## Track your trial

The **Plans** tab shows a trial card:

- *"Your Agency free trial is active · $0 charged today · N days left"*
- **N trial credits remaining** and **Trial ends** *date*
- **No charge scheduled** (no card saved) or **Next charge $X on** *date* (card saved)

We also email you when the trial starts and **3 days before it ends**.

---

## Keep going after the trial

### Add a payment method (recommended)
1. On the trial card, click **Add payment method**.
2. Enter your card and click **Save card**.

Nothing is charged until the trial ends. When it ends, your plan continues automatically:
- You're charged for the first month (shortly after the trial ends).
- You get the plan's monthly credits **plus any trial credits you didn't use**. For example, Pro: 1,900 + 140 unused = 2,040 credits for the first month.

### Start paying now
Once a card is saved, click **Activate plan now** → **Activate plan**. This **ends the trial today** and starts paid billing, and your unused trial credits carry over.

### Switch plan during the trial
With a card saved, click **Upgrade to Agency** or **Downgrade to Pro**. Nothing is charged today, and your trial end date doesn't change.

### Promo code
Enter it in the **Promo code** field on the trial card.

---

## If the trial ends without a card

The workspace is **paused**:
- Every page except **Credits & Billing** is locked.
- Nerve AI, workflows and the Claude/ChatGPT connector stop working.
- You'll see: *"Your free trial has ended. Add a payment method to pay the pending invoice and restore access."*

To restore access, add a payment method. The pending invoice is retried automatically, and your **unused trial credits still carry over**. Access returns within about a minute.

Your connections, workflows and settings are kept.

---

## Cancel your trial

If you don't want to continue, either click **Cancel trial** on the trial card, or just don't add a card. **You won't be charged** unless you add a payment method before the trial ends.

---

## Common questions

**Do I need a credit card to start?** No.

**Will I be charged automatically?** Only if you've added a payment method. Without one, the trial simply ends.

**Can I get a second trial for another workspace?** No. The trial is once per email. Additional workspaces need a paid plan.

**Is there a trial for Enterprise?** No. [Contact us](mailto:hello@mattemena.com) about Enterprise.
