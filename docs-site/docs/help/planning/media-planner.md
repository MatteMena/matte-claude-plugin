# Media planner

The **Media planner** walks you through a campaign brief, then turns it into a prompt that has Claude build a full media plan using your connected account data.

Go to **Planning** → **Media planner** in the sidebar.

---

## Steps

### 1. Pick a vertical
Choose the closest match: **E-commerce / Retail**, **Real Estate**, **Travel & Hospitality**, **SaaS / App**, **Finance / Insurance**, **Automotive**, **B2B / Professional Services** or **Other**.

### 2. Fill in the campaign brief
The brief has four sections:
- **Campaign Basics**
- **Schedule & KPI targets**
- **Channels & Ad Accounts**
- **Brand Research**

Fill in as much as you can; anything you leave out, Claude will ask about or assume.

### 3. Review & generate
Check the brief (click **← Edit brief** to change anything), then:
- **Generate media plan in Claude**: opens Claude with the full prompt, or
- **Copy prompt**: paste it into Claude or ChatGPT.

---

## Tips

- The more specific the brief (budget, dates, KPI targets), the better the plan.
- The Nerve connector must be set up in Claude first. See [Connect Nerve to Claude](../connect-ai/claude.md).
- With the **Claude Code plugin**, Claude can also produce the plan as a formatted Excel file. See [Installation](../../installation.md).
