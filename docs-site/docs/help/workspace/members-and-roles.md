# Members and roles

Invite teammates to a workspace and control what each person can do.

To get there, go to **Settings** in the sidebar → **Members** (*Members & Invitations*).

---

## Roles

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **Viewer** | See everything in the workspace (read-only). Can connect the workspace to their own Claude or ChatGPT. Can't change anything. |
| **Member** | Everything a Viewer can, plus: connect and disconnect ad accounts, chat with Nerve AI, and create, edit, run and delete workflows and templates. |
| **Admin** | Everything a Member can, plus: invite and remove members, and manage billing (plans, payment methods, top-ups, cancelling). |
| **Owner** | Everything an Admin can, plus: change other people's roles. The person who created the workspace is the Owner. |

---

## Invite a teammate

*Owners and Admins only.*

1. Go to **Settings** → **Members**.
2. Click **Invite**.
3. In **Invite team member**, enter their **Email address** and choose a **Role** (**Viewer**, **Member** or **Admin**).
4. Click **Send invitation**. You'll see *"Invitation sent!"*

They get an email invitation. When they [sign up or sign in](../getting-started/sign-up.md) with **that same email**, they join the workspace automatically, with no extra button to click.

### Track invitations
The **Invites** tab lists invitations with **Email**, **Role**, **Status** and **Sent**. To withdraw a pending invitation, click the **X** (**Cancel invitation**).

:::tip Invitation not received?
Ask them to check spam. If it still hasn't arrived, cancel the invitation and send a new one.
:::

---

## Change someone's role

*Owner only.*

1. On the **Members** tab, open the role dropdown next to the person.
2. Choose the new role.
3. Click **Confirm** in the **Change Role** dialog.

---

## Remove a member

*Owners and Admins.*

1. On the **Members** tab, click the trash icon next to the person.
2. Click **Remove** to confirm.

They lose access to the workspace straight away.

---

## Member limits by plan

| Plan | Members per workspace |
|---|---|
| Pro | 1 |
| Agency | 3 |
| Enterprise | Unlimited |

**Pending invitations count toward the limit.** If you see *"Member limit reached. Your plan allows a maximum of N members…"*, remove a member, cancel a pending invitation, or [upgrade your plan](../billing/plans-and-pricing.md).

## Common questions

**Can someone be in more than one workspace?**
Yes. They switch between workspaces with the workspace switcher at the bottom of the sidebar, and can have a different role in each.

**Can a Viewer use Claude or ChatGPT with the workspace data?**
Yes. Any role can connect the workspace to their own AI assistant. Only invite people you trust with your ad account data.

**Can I transfer ownership?**
Not in the app. Email [hello@mattemena.com](mailto:hello@mattemena.com) from the current owner's email.
